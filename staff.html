Front Door Kiosk — setup
  1. Sign up the tablet account. In a private/incognito window, open this app and tap Sign Up. Use an email you control, e.g. you+frontdoor@gmail.com (the +frontdoor part still reaches your normal inbox). Pick a strong password and keep it safe: staff need it to sign the tablet back in.
  2. Give it the kiosk role. Here in Members, open the new account, tick Front Door Kiosk, untick Member, and Save. It can only run the door: no staff access.
  3. Sign in on the tablet. Open kiosk.html on the tablet and sign in with that account. It stays signed in.
  4. Lock it to the screen. Hold the logo for 3 seconds and choose Full screen. On Android, turn on Screen pinning (Settings > Security) so nobody can leave the page, and set the screen timeout to the longest option.
  5. Tell members. They scan the code with their phone, or set a Check-in PIN on My Profile and use their phone number + PIN. Their phone number must be on their profile.

Who gets in: suspended, cancelled, not-set-up, frozen or ended memberships are sent to the front desk. Overdue members are let in twice with a reminder, then blocked until they pay. Staff can always check someone in by hand with Scan Check-in.

Parking — Cars In & Out

The guard parks cars in by plate; the desk checks them out: M-Pesa, cash, or free for members who checked in
Parking — setup
  1. Set the paybill. Open Parking settings at the bottom of this page and enter the gym's M-Pesa paybill number. The rate is KES 20 per hour to start; change it there too. Only an admin can.
  2. Give the guard and desk accounts. Each needs a staff account: in Users, tick Front-Desk Staff on their account. They open Admin > Parking on a phone or tablet.
  3. At the gate: the guard types the plate under Car In and taps Park In.
  4. At the desk: type the plate under Car Out (or tap the car in Parked Now). Read out the fee, paybill and account (the plate). When the M-Pesa SMS arrives, tap Paid by M-Pesa (add the code if you like), or Paid cash.
  5. Members park free when their plate is under My Car Plates on their profile and they checked in at the door that day: a green Exempt button appears. Forgot to check in? Check them in with Scan Check-in in Users first.
  6. Print your Lipa na M-Pesa QR from Safaricom and stick it at the desk, so drivers can scan it instead of typing the paybill.

Fees count every started hour (1h10m = 2 hours). A plate typed wrong at the gate can be removed from its checkout window.

How to run every test in the Initial Assessment, in order, and what to do with the numbers. Log the results in Users > member > Assessment. The same sheet opens inside the Log Assessment form.

0Run sheet: about 45 minutes

  1. Screening: read it before they arrive. Sort out any clearance first (card 1).
  2. Waiver: check it's signed (shown on the screening).
  3. Resting heart rate and blood pressure: first, before any movement (cards 2–3).
  4. Weight, height, tape measurements (cards 4–5).
  5. Posture (card 6).
  6. Movement screen (card 7).
  7. Talk: goals, training history, sleep, stress, food, availability. Most of it is already on the screening; confirm it.
  8. Log it and mark the screening reviewed (card 9).

Reassessments: the same tests, same order, same scale and tape, and the same time of day if you can, so the numbers compare.

1Screening: go / no-go

  • Chest pain, fainting or dizziness, or "supervised exercise only": no training until they bring a doctor's note.
  • Pregnant or gave birth in the last year: get the go-ahead from their doctor or midwife before new or heavy training.
  • Any other yes (heart/BP, a long-term condition, medication, joint problem): ask whether it's under control and whether their doctor knows they're starting. If you're unsure, ask for clearance.
  • Pain or injury now: train around it, don't test into it. If it's sharp, swollen or getting worse, refer them to a physio or doctor.

You're not diagnosing. If in doubt, get clearance.

2Resting heart rate

  1. Sit them down for 5 minutes, quiet, feet flat. Ideally no coffee, smoking or exercise in the 30 minutes before.
  2. Two fingers (not your thumb) on the thumb side of the inner wrist.
  3. Count beats for 30 seconds and double it. Or use the reading from the BP monitor.
  4. Log in Resting HR (bpm).
60–100Normal. Fit people can be in the 40s–50s.
Over 100Rest 5 more minutes and recount. Still over 100: easy session only, and suggest they see a doctor.
Under 50, not fitAsk about dizziness or tiredness. If they have symptoms, refer.

3Blood pressure

  1. Automatic upper-arm cuff, the right cuff size for their arm.
  2. Seated with back supported, feet flat, legs uncrossed. Bare arm resting on the table at heart height.
  3. Cuff 2–3 cm above the elbow crease. No talking while it reads.
  4. Take 2 readings 1–2 minutes apart and log the average, e.g. 128/82.
Under 120/80Normal.
120–139 / 80–89Train as normal. Note it.
140–159 / 90–99Train at moderate effort. Suggest they get it checked.
160+ / 100+No hard training until a doctor clears them.
Over 180 / 120Don't train. Re-measure after 5 minutes. Still that high, or headache, chest pain or blurred vision: get medical help now.

4Weight and height

  1. Same scale every time, on a hard flat floor.
  2. Shoes off, light clothes, pockets empty.
  3. Read to 0.1 kg and note the time of day. Log in Weight (kg).
  4. Height: barefoot, heels, back and head against the wall, looking straight ahead. Flat object on the head, mark the wall, measure to 0.5 cm. Update their profile if it's changed.

5Body fat (tape)

The app uses the US Navy tape method, and falls back to RFM (waist and height only) if there's no neck measurement.

  1. Flexible tape that doesn't stretch, on skin or thin clothing. Snug, not digging in, level all the way round.
  2. Waist: at the navel, relaxed, at the end of a normal breath out.
  3. Neck: just below the Adam's apple, tape sloping slightly down at the front.
  4. Hips (women): the widest point of the buttocks, feet together.
  5. Measure each site twice. If the two differ by more than 0.5 cm, take a third, then use the average.
  6. Put the numbers into Tools > Body Calculators and log the % in Body Fat (%).

6Posture

Relaxed standing, feet hip-width, shoes off. Look from the front, side and back for about 30 seconds each.

  • Front: head tilted, one shoulder or hip higher, knees in or out, feet turned out or flat.
  • Side: head forward of the shoulders, rounded shoulders, big low-back arch or flat back, knees locked back.
  • Back: shoulder blades sticking out, a shift to one side, heels rolling in.

Write down what you see, not a diagnosis: "head forward, L shoulder high".

7Movement screen

Show each one first. Stop if it hurts and note where. Watch from the front and the side.

a. Overhead squat: 5 reps

Feet shoulder-width, arms straight up by the ears, squat as low as is comfortable.

  • Feet turn out, heels lift
  • Knees cave in or push out
  • Leans far forward, arms drop forward
  • Low back arches or rounds
  • Shifts to one side

b. Single-leg balance: 30 s each side

Hands on hips, lift one foot, eyes open.

  • Wobbles or puts the foot down (note how many seconds)
  • Hip drops on the lifted side
  • Standing knee caves in

c. Hip hinge with a dowel: 5 reps

Dowel along the back, touching the head, upper back and tailbone. Push the hips back, soft knees, then stand.

  • Dowel leaves the head or tailbone (back is rounding)
  • Squats it instead of hinging
  • Can't feel the hamstrings

d. Push-up: up to 5 good reps

Floor, knees or hands on a bench: use the hardest version they can do well, and note which.

  • Hips sag or pike
  • Elbows flare wide
  • Head drops, no full lockout

8Stop any test if…

  • Chest pain, pressure or tightness
  • Dizzy, faint, confused, or pale and clammy
  • Much more out of breath than the effort should cause
  • Feeling sick, or a sharp or growing joint pain

Sit or lie them down, stay with them, and recheck pulse and BP. If chest pain doesn't go away, or they faint: call 999 or 112.

9Log it

  1. Users > the member > Assessment > + Log New Assessment.
  2. The first one is pre-filled from their screening. Check every field with them.
  3. Enter weight, body fat %, resting HR and BP.
  4. Movement / Posture notes, short: "OHS knees cave L>R, heels lift · hinge OK · push-up on knees, hips sag · head forward".
  5. Turn on Waiver signed if it is, then Save Assessment.
  6. Press Mark reviewed on their screening.
