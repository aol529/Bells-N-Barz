Bell(e)s n' Barz

Get the app

Your workouts, bookings, front-door check-in, chat with your coach and your progress, on your Android phone.

Download for Android

Version 4.2 · about 4.4 MB · Android 8 or newer

Installing it

  1. Tap Download for Android and open the file when it finishes (or find BellsNBarz.apk in Files > Downloads).
  2. If your phone says it can't install apps from this source, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  3. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect says it doesn't recognise the app, tap More details then Install anyway. That's because the app isn't on the Play Store yet.
  4. Open the app and log in with your gym account, or sign up.

Updates

The app doesn't update itself yet. When the gym tells you there's a new version, come back to this page and install it over the old one: you stay logged in and keep everything.

iPhone?

There's no iPhone app yet. Use the website instead: on your iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen so it opens like an app.
