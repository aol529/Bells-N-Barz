Privacy Policy

Bell(e)s n' Barz gym: the website and the Android app. Last updated 2 November 2026.

This explains what the gym's website and Android app keep about you, why, who can see it, and how to have it deleted. The website and the app share one database, so this applies to both.

What we keep

  • Your account: name, email, phone, date of birth, address, emergency contact, and the details you add to your profile (height, weight, experience, goals, injuries or medical notes, preferred training time).
  • Membership and billing: plan, status, session credits, points, invoices, payments and shop orders. Payments themselves are taken at the front desk or by the payment link you choose; we do not store card or M-Pesa PINs.
  • Training and health logs you choose to add: workout sessions and sets, your program, check-ins, weight, steps, blood pressure, body measurements, progress photos, food log, meal plan answers and check-ins, fasting, cycle (period) log, and the pre-session health screening and waiver.
  • Bookings and messages: classes and 1-on-1 sessions you book, and messages and files you send to coaches, members or groups.
  • Gym operations: front-door check-ins (including a check-in PIN, stored only as a one-way hash) and, if you use the gym's parking, your car's number plate and parking times.
  • Phone alerts: if you allow notifications, a push token for your browser or phone, so the gym can send you booking, message and reminder alerts.

The Android app does not use advertising, does not track you across other apps, and does not read your contacts or location. The camera is used only by Google's barcode scanner when you choose to scan a check-in code, and photos only leave your phone when you choose to upload one.

Why we keep it

  • To run your membership: bookings, check-ins, credits, points, billing and the front door.
  • So your coach can plan your training and nutrition safely (programs, logs, screening, meal plans).
  • To send you messages and alerts you've asked for.

Who can see it

  • You can see everything in your own account.
  • Your coach and the gym's staff can see what they need to coach you and run the gym. Health details, screening answers, food logs and progress photos are limited to your coach and the gym admin.
  • Other members only see what you choose to share: your name in chats you take part in, accountability groups you join, and the monthly leaderboard (you can hide yourself from it in the app).
  • Service providers that store or deliver the data for us: Supabase (database, file storage and sign-in), Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android alerts), and GitHub Pages (hosting the website). They process it only to provide their service. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access is controlled per account in the database, so members can only read their own records.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account is open. If you leave, staff can archive your account (you can no longer sign in) or delete it on request. Billing records may be kept for as long as the law requires.

Deleting your account

In the Android app: More → My profile → Delete my account → Request account deletion. On the website, or if you no longer have the app: send a message to the coaching team in Chat, or ask at the front desk. Staff confirm and delete your account and its data within 30 days, except billing records the law requires us to keep.

Children

The app and website are for gym members and are not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and important changes are announced in the app.

Contact

Questions about your data: message the coaching team in the app's Chat, or speak to the front desk at Bell(e)s n' Barz.
